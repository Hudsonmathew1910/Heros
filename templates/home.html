Privacy & Security

Data Collection & Usage

Your privacy is our top priority. We firmly believe that your data belongs to you. Heros does not collect personal conversation data to train our AI models. Any queries you make are processed strictly for generating your immediate response.

API Key Encryption

All API keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Hugging Face) you provide are immediately encrypted locally using industry-standard AES-256 encryption before being stored on the server. The raw keys are never exposed back to the frontend browser, ensuring they cannot be intercepted by malicious extensions.

Ephemeral Sessions (Zeno)

When you use "Temporary Chat" modes (like the Zeno Extension), your entire chat history is maintained completely in-memory during the active session. Once you close the session, the data is instantly discarded without ever touching our database.

Terms & Policies

Terms of Service

By using Heros, you agree to lawful and responsible use of our AI inference services, respect API rate limits, and maintain the confidentiality of your secret credentials.

Privacy & Zero Training

Heros enforces a strict zero model training policy on user chat prompts, API payloads, and uploaded documents. All third-party provider keys are encrypted locally using AES-256 before storage.

Data Governance & Controls

You retain complete ownership over your data. You can perform real-time request log purges or permanently delete your developer account and workspaces at any time.
